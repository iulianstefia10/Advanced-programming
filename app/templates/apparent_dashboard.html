How it is computed

Heat indexHot and humid air: sweat evaporates less, so it feels hotter. NOAA formula (Rothfusz regression), written in °F, so the app converts °C → °F → °C. Only valid from 27 °C and 40 % humidity.
Wind chillCold and windy air: the wind removes heat from the skin, so it feels colder. Environment Canada formula, wind in km/h at 10 m. Only valid up to 10 °C and above 4.8 km/h.
SteadmanEvery other case. Uses temperature, water vapour pressure (from humidity) and wind in m/s: AT = T + 0.33 e − 0.70 ws − 4.
