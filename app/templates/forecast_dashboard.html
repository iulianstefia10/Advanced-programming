How it works

DataOne value per hour for every variable of every chosen station (Flux aggregateWindow(every: 1h)), holes of up to 3 hours filled by interpolation.
WindowsThe last x hours of all series as input, the next hours of the target as output. Each series is given relative to its last value (its recent changes), and the models predict the change from the last temperature.
SplitBy time, never shuffled: the first 80 % to train, the last 20 % to test, with a gap so no hour is used in both. Scaling is computed on the training hours only.
NaiveThe next hours equal the last known value. A model must beat it to be useful.
Seasonal naiveThe next hours equal the same hours of the previous day (needs a window of at least 24 h and a horizon of at most 24 h).
Linear regressionRidge regression on the target window and the last 6 hours of the other series; the strength of the regularisation is chosen by time on the training windows.
MLPDense network 64 → 32 → horizon with dropout and L2 (TensorFlow), same inputs as the linear model, early stopping on the most recent 10 % of the training windows.
LSTMRecurrent network with 32 units and dropout that reads the whole window of every series hour by hour (TensorFlow), same training.
MAE / RMSEMAE = mean absolute error, the average size of the error in the units of the data (°C, $). RMSE punishes big errors more. Computed on the test hours only; lower is better.
Crypto and forexPrices move almost like a random walk: the past hardly predicts the next change, so the naive forecast is expected to be the best there.
