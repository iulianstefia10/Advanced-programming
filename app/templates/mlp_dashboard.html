Role of each hyperparameter

activationAdds non-linearity. Without it, several Dense layers work like one linear layer.
hidden layers / neuronsSize of the model. Too small underfits, too big is slow and can overfit.
lossWhat the model minimises. It must match the label format (one-hot here).
optimizerHow the weights are updated. adam and rmsprop adapt the step, sgd does not.
batch_sizeImages per weight update. Small = more updates per epoch, big = faster epochs but fewer updates.
epochsPasses over the training data. Too few underfits, too many overfits.
validation_splitPart of the training data kept aside to watch overfitting (Keras takes the last part, without shuffling).
normalizationPuts the pixels between 0 and 1, so the training is stable and converges.
